Fundamental verdict: hold. Siemens Limited is a net-cash industrial-quality franchise, but at about ₹4,022, near its ₹4,098 52-week high, the market is pricing a re-acceleration the last two quarterly prints do not confirm.

Gross margin improved to about 30.5% from 29.1%, but normalised EBITDA margin fell to about 14.9% from 18.1% and operating margin to 9.9% from 12.0%.
